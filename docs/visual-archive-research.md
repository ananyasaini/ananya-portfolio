# Visual Archive Research Notes

Research date: 17 July 2026

This reference log supports Ananya Saini's self-initiated visual archive. References were used to identify broad composition, hierarchy, campaign-system, and presentation patterns. No final design directly copies a referenced layout, identity, image, or client asset.

## Synthesis by category

- **Carousels and social campaigns:** persistent visual hooks, slide-to-slide pacing, one idea per frame, and a closing action rather than isolated decorative tiles.
- **Advertising posters and flyers:** distance-readable typography, deliberate contrast, asymmetric tension, and one dominant message supported by compact event information.
- **Magazine and PDF design:** consistent grids, editorial pacing, expressive opening pages, readable body layouts, and information graphics that belong to the visual system.
- **Emailers:** one clear hero, mobile-friendly modules, strong offer hierarchy, repeated brand language, and restrained calls to action.
- **Thumbnails:** immediate subject recognition, a short editorial proposition, high contrast, and composition designed for 16:9 rather than a cropped poster.
- **Standees:** long-distance reading order, minimal copy, print-safe structure, and brand recognition before detail.
- **Gaming posters:** cinematic light, high-energy diagonals, strict event-information zones, and controlled rather than indiscriminate visual effects.
- **Logo systems:** concept rationale, responsive variations, spacing discipline, palette, typography, and applications—not an isolated mark on a blank page.
